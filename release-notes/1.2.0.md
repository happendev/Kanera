**My Cards** and **Team Cards** now sit at the top of the sidebar and cover every workspace, standalone board and organisation you can access, instead of one workspace at a time. Show the cards as a board, a table, a calendar or a Work done history, narrow them by teammate and board, and select **Save view** to come back to the same filters. **Portfolio** adds counts of active, overdue, unassigned and completed work across boards.

One account can now belong to several organisations. Open the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar to switch between them, create one or join one. Every card also gets a short key such as `PROJ-123`, shown on the card and in card detail and accepted by search.

## Edit and report in Table view

List view is now **Table view**. Select a cell to edit the value in place, drag column edges to resize them and drag headers to reorder them. Grouping, sorting, **Sum** and **Average** totals, **Break summaries down by** and filters work as before.

Use **Export** to download an Excel workbook, JSON or a CSV of the grid exactly as shown. The same table appears as the Table display in My Cards, Team Cards and Portfolio, with a Board column for cards from several boards.

## Send updates to chat and your own channels

Post workspace card activity straight into a team channel. Open **Workspace settings → Integrations**, choose Slack, Discord, Telegram or Zulip, paste the connection your chat tool gives you and pick which events to post, such as new cards, status changes or comments.

For your own notifications, **Settings → Notifications** can now also send to ntfy, Gotify or a personal webhook. On Kanera Cloud, chat destinations and these personal channels need Pro or the Pro trial. Add a workspace rule on the same page to use different notification settings for one workspace or standalone board.

## New features

- [My Cards] My Cards and Team Cards span every workspace, board and organisation you can access
- [Reporting] Portfolio counts active, overdue, unassigned and completed work across boards
- [Home] Home shows overdue, due today, next 7 days and done this week, plus your week
- [Table] List view becomes Table view, with in-place editing and resizable, reorderable columns
- [Organisations] Belong to several organisations with one account and switch from the user menu
- [Cards] Every card has a key such as `PROJ-123`; set the prefix in workspace settings
- [Integrations] Post card activity to Slack, Discord, Telegram or Zulip
- [Notifications] Send your notifications to ntfy, Gotify or a personal webhook
- [Notifications] Workspace rules override your notification settings for one workspace or board
- [Organisations] Organisation owners can delete an organisation from its **Danger zone**
- [MCP] Device sign-in lets a CLI or headless client connect after you approve a code

## Improvements

- [Work done] Each card appears once a day with its move path; copy a period as text
- [Cards] PDF attachments open in a preview, and videos attached to comments play in the viewer
- [Notes] Notes show who last edited them, and open audio and PDF attachments in the viewer
- [Members] The Users page lists pending invitations more clearly and updates as people join or leave
- [Notifications] Notifications name their organisation when you belong to several
- [MCP] Agents can refer to cards by key or link, and manage attachments, covers and reactions

## Bug fixes

- [Notifications] Notifications and unread badges follow a card when it moves to another board

## Self-hosting

- Set `MCP_INTERNAL_SECRET` to a random value of at least 32 characters, different from `JWT_SECRET`, before upgrading. Docker Compose will not start without it.
- To let personal ntfy, Gotify or webhook channels reach HTTP or private-network receivers, set `KANERA_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NOTIFICATION_DESTINATIONS=true`. It is off by default.
