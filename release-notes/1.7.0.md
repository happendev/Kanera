Many confirmation dialogs are gone. When you archive a card, delete an attachment, a note or a list, or remove someone from a board, the change happens straight away and a toast offers **Undo** for ten seconds.

Deletes and access removals wait until the toast closes before they reach the server, so **Undo** puts things back exactly as they were. Archived cards can still be restored from the **Archived** filter after the toast has gone.

## Choose a theme and accent colour

Profile settings now offer four themes: **Dark**, a graphite **Carbon**, **Light** and a warmer **Paper**. Pick an accent colour alongside it to retint buttons, links and focus rings without changing the theme.

Your theme and accent are saved to your account, so every browser and device you sign in on follows them, and an open Kanera tab updates when you change them elsewhere.

## See what your AI agents did

When an AI agent connected to your account changes a card or writes a comment, Kanera now records it as the agent's work done on your behalf. Activity, comments and notifications show your name with a **via** badge naming the agent, and Work done marks agent work with a sparkle.

Agents can also start a run while they work on a card. The board shows a sparkle on that card, and card detail lists the run with its status. A run that has not checked in for 15 minutes is marked stalled. The notification drawer has a new **Agent** tab for everything your agents did.

## Work faster on boards and from the keyboard

The **Quick actions** in the command palette (**Ctrl K**, or **⌘ K** on a Mac) now cover much more: create a card, open the scratchpad, notifications or Up next, switch between light and dark, and jump to Home, My Cards or Team Cards. Each action shows its keyboard shortcut.

On boards, select several cards and drag them to another list in one move. To add a card in a particular spot, use the gap between two cards instead of adding it at the bottom and dragging it up.

## New features

- [Appearance] Carbon and Paper themes and accent colours, synced across your devices
- [Cards] Undo archiving cards and deleting attachments, notes and scratchpad pages
- [Settings] Undo deleting a list, custom field, checklist template, label or automation
- [Members] Undo removing a member from a board or workspace, or a guest from a board
- [AI agents] Agent work is labelled "via" the agent, and live runs show on the card
- [Notifications] An **Agent** tab in the notification drawer collects your agents' activity
- [Automations] New **Post a comment** and **Call a webhook** actions
- [Boards] Add a card between two existing cards
- [Boards] Drag several selected cards to another list at once
- [Calendar] Drag a card to another day to change its due date
- [Boards] Drag boards in the sidebar to change their order
- [Boards] Leave a board yourself from its members menu
- [My Cards] Change the background and use compact cards on My Cards and Team Cards
- [Table] The header row stays in view while you scroll
- [MCP] Agents can create, move and remove separators between cards

## Improvements

- [Settings] Settings are organised into tabs, save as you go and warn about unsaved changes
- [Boards] Bulk card actions confirm what they changed with a toast
- [Home] A refreshed Home that works better on phones
- [Appearance] More consistent empty states, loading states, menus, panels and contrast
- [Scratchpad] Tidier scratchpad and Up next panels, and fewer options in the note menu
- [Performance] Boards load, render and drag cards faster
- [Performance] Table view loads faster and long sessions use less memory

## Bug fixes

- [Boards] Separators with nothing to separate hide while you search or filter
- [Up next] Up next shows a loading state and an empty state
- [My Cards] New cards created from My Cards or Team Cards get their assignee filled in
- [Notifications] Closing the notification drawer returns focus to where you were
- [Security] Fixes for server-side request forgery and authorisation checks
