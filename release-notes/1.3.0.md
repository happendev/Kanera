This release adds **Up next**, a ranked queue of the cards assigned to you across every board, so you and your team can agree on what gets done first without moving cards between lists.

Adding a card now opens a single composer where you can set its details before you create it. Work done gains activity filters, and unread notifications about the same card are grouped together.

## Plan the order of your work with Up next

Open the numbered **Up next** button beside notifications on any page. Rank 1 is the card you plan to work on next. Drag rows to reorder them, add another card assigned to you, mark a card complete, or remove it from the queue without changing the card.

Select **Add to Up next** on a card or Table row to queue it, and its rank then shows on the card. Home shows the top of your queue and your daily digest email includes it. Completing, archiving or unassigning a card takes it out of the queue.

Workspace admins can see and reorder their teammates' queues from Team Cards. While you are offline the queue is hidden, so you never follow an old order.

## Create cards with their details in one step

Adding a card on a board, in Table view or from My Cards now opens one composer. Only a title is needed, but you can also add a description, assignees, labels, a due date, custom field values, checklist templates and attachments before the card exists.

From My Cards, choose the board and list in the composer. Tick **Create another** to keep it open for the next card. Closing the composer keeps what you typed as a draft, and **Cancel** discards it.

## New features

- [Up next] A personal Up next queue that ranks your assigned cards across boards
- [Up next] Workspace admins can view and reorder teammates' queues from Team Cards
- [Cards] A new card composer for setting details and attachments before creating a card
- [Settings] Turn off **Show card keys** in Profile settings to hide keys such as KAN-123
- [Work done] Filter Work done to completed, moved, created or checklist item activity
- [Table] Select every card in a group, and export only the selected cards
- [Notifications] Unread updates on the same card on the same day are grouped into one block
- [Organisations] Switch organisation from a flyout in the organisation menu
- [MCP] Search cards, notes, comments and attachment names across boards in one ranked list
- [MCP] Review one person's work history across projects for stand-ups and one-on-ones
- [Billing] On Kanera Cloud, trial owners see on Home what would change on the Free plan
